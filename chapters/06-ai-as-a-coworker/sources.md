- Dell'Acqua et al., "The Cybernetic Teammate" (P&G field experiment, *Organization Science*): individuals with AI matched teams without it; a next step past Centaurs and Cyborgs. [NBER w33641](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33641) — Kevin's agent
- Microsoft and LinkedIn, 2024 Work Trend Index: 78% of AI users bring their own tools, 52% reluctant to admit using AI on important tasks; large-sample check on p. 144. [microsoft.com](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2024/05/08/microsoft-and-linkedin-release-the-2024-work-trend-index-on-the-state-of-ai-at-work/) — Kevin's agent
- Otis, Clarke, Delecourt, Holtz, and Koning, "The Uneven Impact of Generative AI on Entrepreneurial Performance" (*Management Science*): strong performers gained, struggling ones lost; counterpoint to pp. 156–157. [doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.06909](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.06909) — Kevin's agent
- Brynjolfsson, Chandar, and Chen, "Canaries in the Coal Mine?" (Stanford Digital Economy Lab, updated Aug 2026): early-career employment gap in AI-exposed jobs; tests the p. 155 "little effect" prediction. [stanford.edu](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/) — Kevin's agent
- Yale Budget Lab, "Evaluating the Impact of AI on the Labor Market": no broad disruption so far; the other side of the same question. [budgetlab.yale.edu](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/evaluating-impact-ai-labor-market-current-state-affairs) — Kevin's agent
- Feigenbaum and Gross, "Answering the Call of Automation" (*QJE* 139, no. 3, 2024): the telephone operator story on pp. 155–156, which the chapter's notes don't cite. [academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/139/3/1879/7614605) — Kevin's agent
- METR, "Time Horizon 1.1" (Jan 2026): how long a task AI agents can finish on their own, doubling about every 4 months since 2023; update to pp. 135–136. [metr.org](https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/) — Kevin's agent
- *Thaler v. Perlmutter*, cert. denied March 2, 2026: human authorship requirement stands; update to the p. 133 copyright question. [mayerbrown.com](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/supreme-court-denies-review-in-ai-authorship-case) — Kevin's agent
