# Experiments

*Co-Intelligence* argues you can't understand AI by reading about it — you
have to use it ("bring AI to the table" for everything, and find the **jagged
frontier** of what it can and can't do by probing it yourself). This folder is
where we log those probes.

**To add one:** copy `templates/experiment-log.md` to
`experiments/YYYY-MM-DD--short-name--member.md` and fill it in. Agents may
draft the log; the human ran the experiment and owns the verdict.

## Starter experiments from the book

1. **Map your jagged frontier** — pick five real tasks from your week, hand
   each to your AI, and record where it beat you, matched you, and face-planted.
2. **The all-in week** — invite AI into *every* task for a week (within
   confidentiality limits). Log where it helped, where it slowed you down,
   and what you stopped doing yourself.
3. **Centaur vs. cyborg** — do one task with clean human/AI division of labor
   (centaur), and a similar task fully interleaved (cyborg). Which produced
   better work? Which felt better?
4. **Treat it like a person** — redo a failed prompt after giving the AI a
   persona, context, and feedback, as the book advises. Did it matter?
5. **The worst-AI test** — find something the book says AI can't do well.
   Can the current generation do it now? (Pairs well with `/fact-checker`.)
