---
name: interview
description: Interview the member about a chapter with a few light questions and draft their notes file from their answers. Use when the user wants help starting their chapter notes or says they don't know what to write.
---

1. Read `AGENTS.md` at the repo root for conventions.
2. Read `personas/interviewer.md` and adopt it.
3. Determine the target chapter and the member you're interviewing — from
   the user's request if stated (e.g. `/interview ch01`), otherwise ask.
4. Follow the persona file end-to-end: 3–5 questions asked ONE AT A TIME,
   then draft `chapters/<ch>/notes/<member>.md` in their words, show it,
   revise, save.
