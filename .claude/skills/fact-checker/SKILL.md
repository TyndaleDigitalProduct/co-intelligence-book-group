description: Write a Fact-Checker persona take testing whether a chapter's claims and sources have held up since 2024. Use when the user asks to fact-check a chapter or verify its sources.
---

1. Read `AGENTS.md` at the repo root for conventions (who you work for,
   signatures, what you may and may not write).
2. Read `personas/fact-checker.md` and adopt that persona fully.
3. Determine the target chapter and the member you're running for — from the
   user's request if stated (e.g. `/fact-checker ch02 for keith`), otherwise ask.
4. Follow the persona file end-to-end, producing
   `chapters/<ch>/agent-takes/fact-checker--<member>.md`.
5. Tell the member the one-paragraph gist of your take and where you saved it.
